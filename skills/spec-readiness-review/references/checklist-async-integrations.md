# Async And Integrations Checklist

- Queues, events, jobs, webhooks, streams, callbacks, retries, backoff, ordering,
  idempotency, deduplication, cancellation, timeouts, DLQ, and ack timing.
- Checkpointing, replay, partial failure, duplicate side effects, recovery,
  manual intervention, and final state.
- Provider contracts, credentials, rate limits, fallback, degradation, versioning,
  observability, and support handoff.
- Integration tests, contract tests, fixtures, opt-in external tests, and safe
  local defaults.
