# Admin, Support, And Governance Checklist

- Define admin/support/moderation surfaces, approvals, review queues, overrides,
  impersonation, break-glass access, permissions, separation of duties, tenant
  isolation, reason codes, and change history.
- Specify customer-visible effects, notifications, redaction, evidence,
  escalation, manual intervention, governance, compliance evidence, retention,
  deletion, legal hold, policy decisions, and reporting.
- Test unauthorized admin access, impersonation misuse, audit completeness,
  destructive confirmation, rollback, and support-view redaction.
