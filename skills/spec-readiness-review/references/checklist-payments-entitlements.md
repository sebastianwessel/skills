# Payments And Entitlements Checklist

- Define pricing, plans, trials, subscriptions, one-time/usage billing, credits,
  coupons, tax, invoices, refunds, chargebacks, disputes, and legal terms.
- Specify entitlements, feature gates, limits, grace periods, plan changes,
  downgrades, cancellations, nonpayment, receipts, support, and audit.
- Require provider current docs/API version, webhook signatures, idempotency,
  reconciliation, PCI/payment-data boundaries, privacy, and unhappy-path tests.
